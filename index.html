Connect Google Drive

Clumsy requests access only to files it creates. Your finance data is written to one “Clumsy backup” file in your Drive. Google sign-in is required again when you reopen the app.

To connect: in Google Cloud, enable the Google Drive API, create a Web OAuth client ID, and add this app's JavaScript origin (localhost or HTTPS) to it. Open Clumsy on localhost or HTTPS, not file://. Your client ID is public; never enter a client secret.
